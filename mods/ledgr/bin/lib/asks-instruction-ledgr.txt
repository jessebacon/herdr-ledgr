(separated by " | "). Just above the block, end your prose with exactly one line that counts the
items, such as "ledgr: 3 items for you, 1 blocking" or "ledgr: 1 item for you" (leave out

declare nothing and write neither the line nor the block. Never include suggestions or offers.
